Color Field

One fullscreen triangle. One fragment shader. Every pixel you see is computed from warped noise — there is no image, no geometry, no texture. Scroll to stir the field.

Scroll to stir ↓

01

The canvas is a formula

Unlike EXP.01 — thousands of particles, each a tiny object — this scene contains exactly one triangle stretched past the screen edges. The GPU runs the fragment shader once per pixel, millions of times a frame, and the entire painting is the return value of that function.

02

Domain warping

The fluid look comes from feeding noise into itself: fbm(p + fbm(p + fbm(p))). Each layer bends the coordinate space of the next, so flat noise starts to fold like ink dropped in water.

03

Scroll is a uniform

The page scroll doesn't move the canvas — it is piped into the shader as data, every frame. Progress travels through the field; velocity raises the warp amplitude, so fast scrolling literally stirs harder.

04

The palette

Color comes from a cosine palette: a + b · cos(2π(c·t + d)). Four small vectors define the whole mood of the piece — edit them in src/gl/shaders.ts and the field becomes yours.
